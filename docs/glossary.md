# Glossary

- **Case**: the shared subject of work (a baby, an incident, a new hire). Process instances attach to it and share its facts.
- **Attached process**: one run of a definition on a case, bound to a published version; a label tells two runs of the same definition apart.
- **Work item**: a step, decision or approval as it stands on a case: done, skipped, ready, held, waiting, blocked or upcoming.
- **Task draft**: what someone typed into a task's form and saved for later; it records nothing on the case until the task is completed (0026).
- **Question**: a thread on a work item asking a named person for information it needs, answered by them on the task and listed on their My work (0026).
- **Case record**: every value and file recorded on a case, each with who recorded it, when, and the work item it came from.
- **Instance**: an account seen from the runner; the instance switcher moves between the accounts a user belongs to.
- **Guest**: someone who holds a role in an account without belonging to it, and sees only the forms addressed to them.
- **Process**: a definition of stages, steps, facts, roles, decisions and approvals for achieving an outcome.
- **Definition version**: an immutable, published JSONB document of a process. Each case is bound to one.
- **Change policy**: how a definition changes. Governed definitions publish a version only on an explicit publish; living definitions publish every accepted edit.
- **Draft revision**: one numbered state of a definition's draft, recording the operations that made it, its author and who the author acted for; the only place drafts live.
- **Case binding**: how a process attached to a case follows its definition. A pinned one stays on its version until explicitly moved; a following one moves to each new version.
- **Node operation**: a typed, checked edit to a draft, such as `add_node` or `link`; the one way the editor and the wizard change a definition.
- **Node**: any element of a definition (process, stage, step, fact, role, decision, approval, lookup).
- **Core**: the typed, schema-validated layer of a node: identity, kind, edges, role, actor policy, guards, source, invariants.
- **Content**: the Markdown prose guidance layer of a node.
- **Data**: the open, namespaced extension layer of a node (`bpmn.*`, `excel.*`, `ui.*`, `hooks.*`).
- **Fact**: a typed value recorded once on a case, with who recorded it, when, and its evidence.
- **Lookup**: a named list of values a choice fact is chosen from, each with a stored value, an optional label and code, and whether it is turned off (0025). A process lookup is a node of one definition; a shared lookup belongs to a namespace and is seen by the processes in it and below it.
- **Evidence**: proof backing a fact, such as a document or scan, with format and copy requirements.
- **Guard**: a condition on a step or edge: prose, plus an optional safe expression over facts and roles.
- **Role**: an abstract slot in a definition (for example incident commander), filled by principals at runtime.
- **Role binding**: a `(principal, role, scope)` tuple assigning a principal to a role in an account, namespace or case.
- **Principal**: an actor identity: a user, group or agent, keyed by `(issuer, subject)`.
- **Delegation**: a scoped, expiring grant from a user letting an agent act on their behalf.
- **Account**: the top-level tenant. It owns namespaces, and principals are members of it through account roles.
- **Account role**: a built-in role at account scope: account-owner, account-admin, developer or read-only.
- **Namespace**: a hierarchical path (for example `acme/eng/sre`) in one account that owns definitions, cases and bindings.
- **Namespace permission**: a built-in role at namespace scope, inherited by descendants: namespace-admin, write or read.
- **Permission check**: the `can` question of whether a principal may perform an action on a resource, answered with a reason.
- **Board**: the editor view where a process is built: a column per stage, step and decision cards in them, and links drawn from the facts one step hands to another (0021).
- **Import**: a spreadsheet being turned into processes by the wizard: its workbook, the split into processes, the chat with the authoring agent and its proposals (0024).
- **Split**: the wizard's proposal of which processes a spreadsheet holds, each with the cells it is drafted from, which the person renames, merges or splits before accepting.
- **Proposal**: one agent turn's node operations on a draft, previewed but not saved, which the person accepts as one draft revision or rejects (0011, 0024).
- **Projection**: a view generated from the canonical definition, such as Markdown, BPMN or the graph.
